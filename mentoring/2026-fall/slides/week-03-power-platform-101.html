01 · Why does Power Platform exist?

I had a running compiler and nobody would touch it. They told me computers could only do arithmetic.

Grace Hopper, on the road to COBOL

Her idea, 1950s

Let people write programs in words, not machine code. The people who knew the problem should be able to tell the computer what to do.

Same idea, today

That’s still the idea behind the Power Platform. This week’s read, Broad Band by Claire L. Evans, tells her story.

01 · The gap Power Platform closes

The people who know the problem can’t always build

Every team has a wish list

A tracker. An approval. A report that doesn’t take three days of copy-paste. Most of it never gets built.

IT can’t get to it

Developers are busy with the big systems. The small, important stuff waits in a backlog.

So you close the gap

Low-code puts building in the hands of the person who understands the work, with guardrails IT can trust.

80%
of low-code tool users sit outside formal IT, by 2026

Gartner’s forecast for this year. Most people building on these platforms aren’t professional developers. They’re people like us.

03 · Where is Power Platform going?

Power Platform, from forms to agents

2015
Power BI goes GA. Dashboards for everyone, not just analysts.
2016
PowerApps and Microsoft Flow go GA. Apps and automation without code.
2019
Flow becomes Power Automate. Power Virtual Agents brings chatbots.
2020
Dataverse gets its name. Desktop automation arrives via Softomotive.
2021
Power Fx: an open-source, Excel-style formula language.
2022
Power Pages goes GA. Secure websites, low-code.
2023
Copilot arrives. Power Virtual Agents becomes Copilot Studio.
2025
Describe an app and a team of agents builds it. Agent 365 to govern them.
2026
Agents everywhere. One always-on roadmap. Apps built inside Copilot.

03 · Where Power Platform is heading

Three shifts worth betting on

Shift 1

Drag and drop → describe it

You explain what you need in plain language. Copilot builds the first draft. Your job moves from building every screen to knowing what good looks like.

Shift 2

Apps → agents

Software that doesn’t wait to be clicked. Agents read, decide and act, and hand off to a person when they should.

Shift 3

Makers vs devs → one team

Low-code makers and pro developers now work on the same platform, with the same source control and the same AI tools.

And governance is the thing that makes it scale

Admins can see every app, flow and agent, set the guardrails, and still let people build. That’s why big companies say yes to it.

What’s being built right now

Agents, your way

Copilot Studio builds agents, workflows and agent flows. Pick the runtime: instructions-first, classic topics, or an extension of Copilot Chat.

Apps by describing them

Plan designer and a team of agents draft the app for you. App building is now arriving inside Copilot Cowork and Copilot Studio.

Pro code, same platform

Code apps in React or Vue, Dataverse MCP servers, a Python SDK, and deploy from Git.

Governance for agents

Agent 365 as the control plane. Agent security controls and real-time risk checks in Copilot Studio.

No more release waves. Since September Microsoft ships continuously on one roadmap: aka.ms/AIatWorkRoadmap

03 · Hold on to this

The tools change every six months. These don’t.

Understanding the problem

Who’s stuck, what it costs, and what “done” looks like.

Process thinking

Steps, owners, exceptions. Agents need this more than apps did.

Data sense

Where the data lives, who owns it, and whether you can trust it.

Security and trust

Who should see what. The question that gets a project approved.

Getting people to use it

Adoption, training and telling the story. Most projects live or die here.

If AI writes more of the build, these are what you’re paid for.

04 · What does Power Platform mean for my career?

You don’t need to become a developer.

You need to become the person who can turn a problem into something that works.

04 · Power Platform career map

Eight doors in. Most don’t say “developer”.

Maker in your role

Stay where you are and fix how your team works. The fastest start.

From any role

Functional consultant

Translate what the business needs into what gets built.

From ops, HR, finance

Data analyst

Turn messy data into decisions with Power BI.

From reporting, Excel

Developer

Extend with code, components, APIs and pipelines.

From IT, engineering

Solution architect

Design across apps, data, security and integration.

From any of the above

Platform and CoE lead

Run governance, adoption and the maker community.

From IT admin, PMO

Agent builder

Design, test and run AI agents people trust.

Fastest growing

Advocate and educator

Teach, speak, build community. Visibility is the job.

From teaching, comms

Careers here zigzag. Pick the next door, not the last one. Most people walk through two or three of these.
